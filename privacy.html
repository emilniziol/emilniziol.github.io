Privacy Policy

Reflex Stack · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Reflex Stack (package com.gigihiu.reflexstack), published by gigi hiu (“we”), handles information. We keep it simple: the game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and we do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, diamonds, best scores and settings such as sound) is saved locally on your device only. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game diamonds. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • the Android advertising ID,
  • device information (model, operating system version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data (for example ad views, taps, crashes of the ad SDK).

This data is processed by Google, not by us, and is transmitted in encrypted form. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy button on the game’s About screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Watching ads is always optional. The game can be played without them.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

5. Children

Reflex Stack is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. Because the game may still appeal to younger players, ad content is limited to a maximum rating of PG (parental guidance). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will help remove it.

6. Data deletion

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove local game data, uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings. To manage data held by Google for advertising, use your Google account settings or reset your advertising ID as described above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact
