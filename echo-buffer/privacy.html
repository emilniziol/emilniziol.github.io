Privacy Policy

Echo Buffer · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Echo Buffer (package com.gigihiu.EchoBuffer), published by gigi hiu (“we”), handles information. The game has no email or password sign-in and no chat.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, best scores, level, missions, achievements and settings) is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

2. Nickname and leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

To show world leaderboards, the game signs you in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). Unity creates a random player ID. The following is stored on Unity’s servers:

  • the random player ID,
  • the nickname you choose. It is shown publicly in the leaderboards and kept so that no other player can use it. Please do not use your real name. Nicknames with offensive words are not accepted,
  • your scores.

No email, phone number or real-world identity is required. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner during play and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for in-game coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • the Android advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data.

This data is processed by Google and sent encrypted. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Watching rewarded videos is always optional. The game asks ad networks to show only content suitable for all ages.

5. Notifications

Optional daily reminders are scheduled on your device (local notifications). No data is sent for them. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your phone settings.

6. Sharing

“Share” and “Challenge” only open your phone’s share menu with a short text that you choose to send.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

8. Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to remove local data. To delete your nickname and leaderboard entries, email us with your nickname and we will remove them within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
