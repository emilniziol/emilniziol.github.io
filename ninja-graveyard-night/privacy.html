Privacy Policy

Ninja: Graveyard Night · Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Ninja: Graveyard Night (package com.gigihiu.ninjanoc), published by gigi hiu (“we”), handles information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no leaderboards and no chat.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades, heroes and skins, missions, achievements, daily challenge times and settings) is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it. We do not receive a copy.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game has no banners and no forced ads. It only shows optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to revive, to start a level with a bonus or to double a reward). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • the Android advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data.

This data is processed by Google and sent encrypted. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads consent.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Watching rewarded videos is always optional. The game asks ad networks to show only content suitable for a general audience.

4. Notifications

Optional reminders (at most one per day, for example about the daily reward) are scheduled on your device (local notifications). No data is sent for them. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone settings.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

6. Children

The game contains cartoon fantasy violence (a ninja fighting zombies and orcs) and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact us.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstall the game or clear its app data to remove everything stored by the game. We hold no other data about you.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
